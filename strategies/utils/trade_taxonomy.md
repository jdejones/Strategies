Unless specified "Market Open" means early after the market opens. Maybe in the first 15 minutes at most. Some discretion can be used to define it.
Intraday timeframe refers to a 3-minute timeframe. A default is needed for assigning lookback to moving averages. Other timeframes can be used but the moving average's lookback may vary.
# Long
## Momentum
### 1 day lookback
#### Daily timeframe
- News with revenue impact preferred. May be forward looking. Some breaking news required
- Gap minimum 5%
- RVol > 2 (in real-time will be measured on intraday timeframe)
- ATRs Traded > 1.5, 2 preferred
#### Intraday timeframe
- Pre-market ange breakout
- Opening range breakout
- Gap up double bottom w/ 9EMA support
- Mid-day perk
- Late-day rally
- Mid-Late day below VWAP 5_9EMA cross
- 9EMA VWAP cross

- On first day, RVol > 2

#### Intraday timeframe
- Elevated RVol, greater than first day preferred
- Support immediately above prior day high of day
- Support at 2D AVWAP
- Mid-Late day below VWAP 5_9EMA cross

## Breakout
### Daily timeframe
- Markup 30%-100% within the last 1-3 months. The markup should take 3 or more days 
- Orderly retracement
- Higher lows within consolidation patterns
- Consolidation could be expected to last ~2 weeks to ~2 months
- High volume break above resistance
- There may be some small retracements after consolidation has developed that preclude breakouts and are worth watching
### Intraday timeframe
- Elevated RVol
- Trading above high within the consolidation period
- Buyers aggressive above VWAP
- 9EMA support

## Episodic Pivot
### Daily timeframe
- Gap > 8%
- Close > 20DMA
- Episodic pivot status remains across periods while Close > 20DMA
### Intraday timeframe
- Support above prior resistance. Particularly from the first few days
- Mid-late day 5_9EMA crossover below vwap
- Support below AVWAP from the first day
- Support on days that overlap the 20DMA

## Fundamental
Fundamental data can give reason to watch a stock for technical conditions that confirm or deny the fundamental conditions. Fundamental data can also reinforce (confirm) reason to participate in favorable technical trades.
Can use quant rating to aggregate fundamental metrics for comparison
- Micro Cap <300M
- Small Cap 300M-2B
- Mid Cap 2B-10B
- Large Cap 10B-500B
- Mega Cap >500B
### All long trades
Mostly reinforce reason for participating.
- Q/Q increasing Revenue, preferred
- Income > 0, preferred
### Value breakouts
Can create conditions for watching technical conditions that confirm or deny fundamental conditions.
- Insider buying
- Institutional Accumulation
- Low P/E and/or P/S

## Capitulation
### Daily timeframe
- Aggressive selling typically initiated by strong bearish catalyst
- Increasing RVol/Volume and range expansion into decline
- Price meets level of support
- Gap lower
### Intraday timeframe
- Premarket range (high of range) break at open
- 5_9EMA crossover
- "Right-side of the V"/support, then test

## News Alerts
New information, not priced in, introduced to the market. Condition sets will be related to activity and volatility related to the time of release.
### Macro
- Related to breaking news
- Economic data releases
- Legislation
- Geopolitical events

### Breaking news
- New information, maybe unexpected
- Typically has direct impact on revenue


# Short
## Capitulation
### Daily timeframe
- Strong buying pressure, often secular/uncorrelated
- Weak or no catalyst
- Range expansion with elevated RVOl into an exhaustion gap (gap larger than all prior during markup)
### Intraday timeframe
- Opening Print Break (OPB) break of premarket low
- Support break at open
- Aggressive buying at open with sharp reversal

## Breakdown
### Daily timeframe
- Recent markup, >50%
- Distribution when range bound
- Catalyst the denies the reason for initial markup, preferred
- High volume break below support
### Intraday timeframe
- Elevated RVol
- Resistance at prior interday support
- Resistance at 9EMA
- Resistance at VWAP
- 9EMA VWAP cross

## Momentum
### Daily timeframe
- Preceded by high prices. The markup doesn't have to be recent, but enough to allow for long period of negative momentum
- Typically aligned with declining industry/sector
- The catalyst is often unfavorable disruption
- Declining mid-long term moving averages
- Observable/established trend
### Intraday timeframe
- Break below prior support
- Resistance at prior support
- Resistance at 9EMA
- Resistance at VWAP
- Break below inraday support
- 5_9EMA crossover above VWAP

## Fundamental
- Large cap companies with no income
- Companies marked up with no income
- Institutional/insider distribution/selling
- Earnings miss
- Earnings revision lower

## Macro
- Related to breaking news
- Economic data releases
- Legislation
- Geopolitical events

## Breaking News
- New information, maybe unexpected
- Typically has direct impact on revenue
